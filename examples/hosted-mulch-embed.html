Check the example

  1. Choose square feet / inches.
  2. Enter 100 sq ft and a new 3 in layer.
  3. Choose 2 cubic feet printed on each bag.

100 × 3 ÷ 12 = 25 cu ft
25 ÷ 27 ≈ 0.926 cu yd
25 ÷ 2 = 12.5, rounded up to 13 whole bags

These are test inputs, not a recommended depth or package size. No extra allowance, supplier minimum, price, or delivery fee is included.
