Formula

Rectangle volume = length × width × depth. Planned volume = measured volume × (1 + allowance ÷ 100). Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27.

This is a planning check, not a supplier quote or material prescription. Confirm the label, finished depth, order increment, and delivery rules before buying.
